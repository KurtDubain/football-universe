## English Summary

A **pure-frontend, observer-style football simulator** with 32 default clubs, three league tiers and six cup competitions: League Cup, Super Cup, Mainland Cup, Southern Cup, Eastern Cup and World Champions Cup. Cups follow different seasonal schedules. Press "Advance" and witness:

- 🏆 Dynasties rise and crumble across infinite seasons
- 💥 Underdog cup runs and shocking upsets
- 👔 Coaches get fired under pressure, then resurrect at new clubs
- 📰 A deterministic Narrative Director connects causes, turning points, and long-term consequences
- 📈 Team OVR drifts naturally with mean reversion
- 🎲 Seeded simulation — reproduce results with the same version, seed, settings and simulation-affecting choices

**4 game modes**: Free / Epic Dynasty / Underdog Rise / Sandbox (custom teams).
Separately, choose one of **3 guided observer lenses** or a neutral observer view; these are not additional game modes.
**30+ achievements**, season narratives and a multi-season chronicle with bounded detailed history. PWA installation and core offline play are available after the first online load; secondary pages must be visited online before offline reuse.

## Features | 核心特性

### 🏟️ 完整联赛体系
三级联赛（16+8+8），双循环赛制，升降级 + 中立场单回合附加赛。冠军/降级实时概率、积分走势折线图、收官之战标签。

### 🏆 杯赛体系
共有六项具名杯赛：联赛杯、超级杯、大陆杯、南洲杯、东洲杯和环球冠军杯，按下表的不同周期举办，并非每季全部举行。

| 赛事 | 赛制 | 频率 |
|------|------|------|
| **联赛杯** | 32队中立场单回合淘汰 | 每赛季 |
| **超级杯** | 16队主客场小组赛 + 两回合淘汰，决赛中立场 | 每赛季 |
| **大陆/南洲/东洲杯** | 区域内全队参赛；俱乐部积分用于分档，中立场3轮小组赛 + 单回合淘汰 | S5起每6赛季 |
| **环球冠军杯** | 32队、轮换东道主、8组中立场3轮小组赛 + 单回合淘汰 | 每4赛季 |

经典淘汰赛晋级图 / 对阵列表切换 · 移动端当前轮自动定位 · 客场进球规则 · 杯赛规则卡 · 9 段原创赛事音乐与跨页面播放会话

### 🎮 4 种游戏模式
- **自由模式** — 默认平衡设置
- **王朝模式** — 顶级豪门更强，王朝难破
- **草根逆袭** — 实力均衡，弱队多冷门
- **沙盒模式** — 配合自定义球队编辑器

开局观察视角另分为豪门守成、挑战者、草根长征三个引导路线，以及不绑定球队的纯观察。视角决定关注起点，不等同于调整模拟参数的游戏模式。

### ⚽ 深度模拟引擎
泊松分布进球采样 + 多因素加权：OVR · 实际首发 · 教练能力 · 自动阵型与策略 · 士气 · 疲劳 · 主场 · 动量 · 德比。弱队通过有代价的低位防守或快速反击应战，不获得隐藏固定补正。世界杯东道主拥有独立赛会氛围，不与普通主场优势混用。**Seeded PRNG**：相同版本、种子、初始配置和影响模拟的操作序列可复现结果；只分享种子不保证不同版本或干预后的世界一致。

### 📺 Canvas 2D 比赛直播
22 名真实比赛阵容球员实时传球跑动 · 四种真实阵型与五种比赛策略 · 焦点球员克制标记 · 高 DPI 固定帧率画布 · 扑救/封堵/射偏定向演出 · 加时与点球阶段 · 可选声音 · 文字解说

### 👔 教练生态
36 名教练 × 5 种风格 × 4 种首选阵型 · 根据实力差、体能与赛事阶段自动选择策略 · 预测/赛果/回放冻结同一部署 · 压力下课 + 合同到期 + 急流勇退 · 名帅殿堂排行榜

### 球星观察
世界焦点完整收录所有达标现役球员 · U23 新星雷达 · 最多关注 8 名球员并跨转会追踪 · 每场直播最多 5 名真实首发焦点 · 四个位置共用本场影响与最佳球员通道，不公开隐藏潜力或额外“球星分”

### 世界叙事导演
比赛日“世界脉搏”以 1 条主故事 + 最多 2 条短信号控制注意力，其余去重事实默认折叠。联赛不败、杯赛巨人杀手、四位置球员近况、换帅反弹、重大转会、争冠升降级、洲际舞台和可靠纪录都来自现有权威数据；“故事脉络”、赛季版图、实体档案线、赛季回顾与历史摘要共用稳定 arc 身份。叙事层不消费模拟 RNG，也不写入或改判比赛结果。

### 📊 长期历史 + 编年史
赛季回顾（含叙事文案）· 历史奖杯榜 · 趣味纪录 · **30+ 成就**（首次类/纪录类/数据类/王朝类/黑马类）· 编年史多赛季回顾 · 跨赛季交手记录。为控制浏览器存档体积，完整交手、球员赛季数据和球队赛季记录分别保留最近 3、25、40 个赛季，奖杯与核心荣誉继续长期累计。

本地人物追溯改进（尚未发布）：上季金球、金靴、最佳后卫和最佳门将延续一季焦点资格，默认十席最多保障四位不同得主，其余席位沿用表现与能力排序。关注人物在新季无数据时展示已知荣誉身份；整季摘要提炼冠军变化、个人成就和球队去向。球员档案最多展示四个关键节点，当前伤停不会被固定历史节点挤掉。退役记录保留最近300人，另为个人奖项得主和关注人物保留精简事实，不再复制俱乐部完整奖杯历史；摘要不等于完整比赛回放，已清理的旧记录不会伪造恢复。

### 🎲 互动元素
赛季预测（冠军/降级）· 每轮观察判断（胜平负/进球区间/爆冷）· 上帝之手（每赛季 1 次球队 buff/debuff）· 手动解雇关注球队教练。预测与判断不消耗资源，也不改变模拟结果。

### 🌍 大洲对抗系统
3 大洲（大陆/南洲/东洲）跨洲赛事统计 · 三层德比体系（巅峰/焦点/地区，OVR动态判定）

本地运行：

```bash
git clone https://github.com/KurtDubain/football-universe.git
cd football-universe
pnpm install
pnpm dev      # http://localhost:5173
pnpm build    # 个人版生产构建 → dist/personal
```

Current release: **v4.82.0**, the round that makes the top of the season table comparable across positions, **merged to `main` by fast-forward from `feat/tail-calibration` (2 commits, `4e5e7d3..5524d50`, linear history, no merge commit), pushed to `origin/main` (`4e5e7d3..5524d50`) and deployed** to https://football.dyp02.vip/ as `v4.82.0-5524d50-20261127` (switched 2026-11-27 10:30 Beijing time), with `v4.81.0-7ae209e-20261127` retained as `previous` for rollback. One part, and it changes how a season score is computed, so it changes who wins the awards; it does not change the simulation. **A single slope could only pin one quantile, and the one it pinned was the P95.** Every score version up to here fitted one slope per position × tier, which lands each group on 50 at its own median and on 82 at its own P95 before the value offset — and says nothing at all about what happens above that. What happened above it was set by how wide each group's raw tail happened to be, which is not a property anyone chose: measured on 231,866 season rows of 900 minutes or more across 600 completed seasons, how far a group climbs above its own P95 is its raw span from P95 to P99.9 times its scale, and the scale is solved backwards from "land this group on 82 at P95", so it shrinks as the raw distribution widens and the product was never equal across groups: the top flight reads keeper 8.97 × 1.848 = 16.6 climb with a +0.4 offset landing on 99.0, defender 7.90 × 3.387 = 26.8 with −7.0 landing on 101.8, midfielder 9.38 × 3.365 = 31.6 with −1.2 landing on 112.4, and forward 13.20 × 2.214 = 29.2 with +7.8 landing on 119.0. The keeper is last not because his raw tail is narrow — at 8.97 it is wider than the defender's — but because his scale is the smallest in the table, so the widest raw distribution buys the least distance; the forward is first because a 29.2 climb then has the largest offset in the table added to it at full strength, and that offset was solved for the middle of the distribution and never meant to be read at the extreme. **The season score at P99.9 read 104.1 for a forward against 92.6 for a keeper, and no goalkeeper in the whole sample ever reached 100.** The best one read 96.6 while forwards and midfielders both reached 107.5, which is what a reader sees as "forwards and midfielders are over a hundred, defenders and keepers are in the eighties". **The calibration now has a second segment above the P95.** Everything at or below the knee is untouched — same medians, same scales, same value offsets, so every row in the bottom ninety-five percent of its group scores exactly what it scored under version 9 and the curve is continuous where the two segments meet. Above the knee a second slope is solved so that every top-flight group lands on a common absolute 112 at its own P99.9, which is the position quality at which a season score crosses 100 at the confidence an elite row actually carries. The target is deliberately absolute rather than 82 plus each position's offset: an offset that answers "what is this position worth on average" is the right question across the bulk of the distribution and for filling a top twenty, and the wrong question at the extreme, where the reader is asking whether the best season anyone had at this position belongs in the same conversation as the best season at another. So the offset still decides where each position's P95 sits and fades to nothing by P99.9. A lower division inherits its top flight's tail slope for the same reason the first segment is capped — an uncapped third-tier defender tail solves to 10.26 against the top flight's 4.71. **Measured on the same 231,866 rows**, the top-flight season score at P99.9 goes from 92.6 / 93.7 / 100.6 / 104.1 to 101.2 / 100.4 / 100.5 / 100.3 — a spread of 11.5 down to 0.9 — and each position's best season goes from 96.6 / 101.0 / 107.5 / 107.5 to 107.1 / 106.8 / 107.5 / 106.9. All twelve groups' positional-quality medians and P95s are unchanged to the decimal. The season-score top twenty moves toward its 1:2:3:3 design target, defender 17.4% → 19.0% and forward 37.9% → 37.1% against targets of 22.2% and 33.3%, so POSITION_VALUE_OFFSET is deliberately left alone — the residual is the seed-noise floor the source already documents, where solving on three seeds hits the target exactly and reading the same four numbers on ten held-out seeds lands five points off. **The cost is stated rather than hidden:** the forward's extreme top is compressed, top-flight forward positional quality at P99.9 falling 119.2 → 112, and the Ballon d'Or winner's forward share falls from 45.0% to 33.3% on 60 samples (a standard error near six points, so this is a direction and not a magnitude). Real Ballons d'Or went to a forward 63.8%–72% of the time between 1956 and 2025, so this round moves that one number further from reality, not closer. The all-time award leaderboard is unchanged — fifteen forwards, two midfielders, two keepers and one defender in the top twenty — because its bias is structural: two of the seven awards are locked to forwards, so a forward has twice the opportunities before a single score is read. PLAYER_SCORE_VERSION goes from 9 to 10. SAVE_SCHEMA_VERSION stays at 26 and no save field was added or renamed.
